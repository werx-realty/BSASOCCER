The Program

DEVELOP THE
WHOLE PERSON

The BSA Pro Residency Program is a high-performance soccer and education environment built around one simple idea: develop the whole person first. Our model blends elite soccer training with an online homeschool platform, daily mentoring, and a professional standard in everything we do.

01
🧠
The Human
Character First
We develop people before players.
  • Character, humility, and leadership
  • Respect on and off the field
  • Daily standards for attitude & effort
02

Education Creates Options
Academics come before soccer in our priorities.
  • Academic excellence commitment
  • Homeschool & online learning support
  • Discipline and time management
03
⚡

  • High-intensity, position-specific training
  • Small-sided & game-realistic environments
  • Individual development plans
